Yesterday’s News

Yesterday, I woke up before dawn. I wanted to be early on my first day at work. I quickly showered, dressed and headed to the kitchen for breakfast.

“You know, I got my first job when I was twelve too,” Grandpa said when I entered the kitchen.

“I didn’t know you were awake. Why are you up so early?” I asked.

“I wanted to help you get ready for work,” he said. “I think it’s a good thing that you got this job.”

Just then, my mom came in. “Good morning,” she said. “Listen, Leona. I don’t know if this is such a good idea. You’re only 12. Why do you want to go to work at 5 o’clock in the morning?”

“Mom, we’ve talked about this. I want to go to band camp this summer. How are we going to afford it if I don’t work? Besides, all I have to do is ride around on my bicycle and deliver newspapers to restaurants and businesses.”

“I know,” said Mom. “But it’s so dark out there, and…”

“Listen, Elisha,” Grandpa interrupted. “I know you’re worried. But Leona here is one of the most responsible kids in town. She may just be 12, but she’s as mature as all the mostly-grown teenagers running around. This is good for her.”

I looked at my mom and my grandpa. They both worked hard every day so we could live in our little house and keep food on the table. I knew it wasn’t easy for them. I wanted to help out. And I wanted to go to band camp this summer!

“Mom, you know me and you know I can handle it. It’s going to be a piece of cake. Can you help me pack a lunch, though? Today they’re serving meatloaf at school. Yuck.”

Mom laughed and rubbed my head like I was still a little girl. Then she started making a giant sandwich with last night’s leftover roast beef. Yum!

Today, I’m up again before dawn. It turns out delivering newspapers isn’t all that hard. It sure is early in the morning, though!
